        About High & Low

        High & Low

        A private mood tracker designed for individuals navigating mood cycles.

        100% Private & On-Device

        All your data stays directly on your device. There are no accounts and no outside servers.

        No Advertisements, No Tracking

        Zero advertisements, zero analytics, zero tracking cookies, and zero commercial manipulation.
