# High & Low

**High & Low** is a local-first, zero-friction mood tracker designed specifically for individuals navigating bipolar and unipolar mood cycles (both depressive lows and manic/hypomanic highs).

---

## What It Is

High & Low is a lightweight, offline-capable web application built to make mood tracking effort-free—especially on severe low-energy days. It uses simple 1–5 rating scales and binary Yes/No check-ins presented one card at a time, keeping cognitive load to a minimum.

---

## Key Features

- **Low-Energy Tracker Deck**: Single-card question flow with full-width tap targets designed for quick, low-effort check-ins.
- **"Skip the Rest" Escape Hatch**: Complete a check-in instantly at any point without feeling guilty or leaving corrupted data.
- **Mood Timeline & Analytics**: Clean visual trend graphs plotting active questions over time on a unified scale.
- **Custom Question Library**: Create, tag, and organize custom questions with customizable response curves (`more-is-better`, `less-is-better`, `middle-is-best`) or Yes/No toggles.
- **100% Private & Local-First**: All data is stored on-device in IndexedDB. No accounts, external servers, or tracking cookies.
- **Ergonomics & Handedness Support**: Configurable left- or right-handed drawer positioning, dark/light theme switching, and high-contrast modes.
- **Full Data Ownership**: Export or import your entire tracking history anytime as JSON with smart merge support.

---

## Why It Exists

Most mood tracking tools demand high cognitive effort: complex 1–10 rating scales, mandatory journal entries, clutter, or cloud accounts. During intense depressive lows or hyperactive manic phases, these barriers often cause tracking habits to collapse entirely.

High & Low exists to eliminate that friction. By prioritizing rapid tap choices, touch-safe ergonomics, forgiving skip options, and strict privacy, High & Low ensures that tracking remains accessible when you need it most.
