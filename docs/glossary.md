# High & Low Glossary

The English words High & Low uses for its main ideas, and the impact each one is meant to have on the person reading
it. Translators: you're welcome to find a different word, as long as it leaves the reader feeling the same way. See
`docs/translation-guide.md` for the bigger picture.

| English                  | Intended impact on the user                                                                                                                                                                   |
|:-------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Check-In**             | A small, light moment of noticing how you are right now. It should feel quick and self-directed, like something you do for yourself, not something you are called to or have to complete.     |
| **Entry**                | The quiet record a check-in leaves behind: one mark on your timeline. Neutral and unobtrusive, with nothing to maintain or catch up on.                                                       |
| **Mood Tracker**         | The place where you check in. It should feel like a calm, familiar spot to land, not a dashboard or a form.                                                                                   |
| **Question**             | An invitation to say how something is for you. It is never a test, so there is nothing to get right.                                                                                          |
| **Score**                | Just a way of turning a feeling into a number, so it can be seen over time. It is not a grade, and no number is better or worse to give.                                                      |
| **Skip / Skip the Rest** | Permission to stop at any point, with no cost. Skipping should read as an ordinary, respectable choice, never as giving up or leaving something unfinished.                                   |
| **Note**                 | An optional space to say more, in your own words. It should feel open and unpressured, and never something expected.                                                                          |
| **History / Timeline**   | A gentle look back at how you've been over time. It should feel like a picture to explore at your own pace, not a report card or a record of performance.                                     |
| **Question Catalog**     | Your shelf of questions, which you can arrange to suit you. It should feel like a personal, flexible library, not a fixed set of requirements.                                                |
| **Built-in / Custom**    | A simple, neutral way to tell the app's own questions from the ones you wrote. Neither is better, and custom questions should feel just as welcome.                                           |
| **Removed**              | Set aside, not destroyed. Removed questions can be brought back, and the word should reassure rather than alarm.                                                                              |
| **Backup**               | Looking after your own data, and being able to recover it if something goes wrong. It should feel like quiet peace of mind.                                                                   |
| **Smart Merge**          | The safe choice. It should sound careful and protective: nothing you already have will be lost.                                                                                               |
| **Wipe & Replace**       | The weighty choice. It should sound clear and serious without being frightening, so nobody does it by accident but nobody feels scolded for considering it.                                   |
| **Hold to Confirm**      | A small safeguard against accidental taps on actions that matter. It should feel like the app quietly looking out for you, not like it doesn't trust you or is making you jump through hoops. |
